4. Media optimization, encryption, and deletion

Spacewise always lets you inspect items before any destructive action occurs:

  • Photos and videos deleted from your library are moved by iOS to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app for roughly 30 days. Storage is reclaimed once that album is emptied by you or iOS.
  • Media Optimizer & Video Compression create new optimized still, HEIC, or compressed video copies in your Photos library first; the heavier originals are only moved to Recently Deleted if you confirm removing them.
  • Contacts are merged or deleted only after your confirmation, and Spacewise writes a local .vcf backup beforehand (restorable via Settings › Contact backups).
  • Encrypted Secret Folder items are encrypted on-device with 256-bit AES-GCM and excluded from backups. Forgotten PINs cannot be recovered, and deleting the Spacewise app permanently erases all items inside the Secret Folder. Always export items back to Photos before deleting the app or changing devices.
