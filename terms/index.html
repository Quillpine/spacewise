4. Deleting data is your decision

Spacewise identifies files that are typically safe to remove and shows you every item before anything happens. You choose what gets deleted and you confirm it. Please note how deletion actually works:

  • Photos and videos are moved to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for roughly 30 days. Storage is only reclaimed once that album is emptied, by you or by iOS. This is an iOS behaviour that no app can bypass.
  • Contacts are merged or deleted only after you confirm. The app writes a .vcf backup beforehand, which you can restore from Settings › Contact backups.
  • Calendar events you select are deleted through iOS and may also be removed from any account that syncs that calendar.
  • Secret Folder items are stored inside the app. Deleting the Spacewise app deletes them permanently. Export anything you want to keep back to your photo library first.
  • Video compression saves a smaller copy. The original is only removed if you choose to remove it.
