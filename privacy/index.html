Spacewise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Spacewise is an offline photo curation and media optimization studio for iPhone, published by Quillpine. This policy explains how Spacewise handles your information. The short version: Spacewise operates 100% offline on your iPhone and collects zero personal data. There is no account, no cloud server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind.

Summary

  • We do not collect, transmit, store, or receive any personal data.
  • Spacewise makes zero network connections. Everything runs locally on your iPhone.
  • Your photos, videos, and contacts never leave your device.
  • Items placed in the Secret Folder are encrypted at rest on your iPhone using 256-bit AES-GCM (Apple CryptoKit).
  • The app contains no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

1. Who we are

Spacewise is developed and published by Quillpine ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, write to trankien.sk.1211@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Quillpine does not operate any backend server for Spacewise, does not receive any telemetry or media from the app, and has no way to identify you or your device. We do not build profiles, we do not sell or share data, and we do not display advertising.

3. Data the app accesses on your device

Spacewise requests iOS permissions only when you open the feature that requires them. You can decline any permission and continue using the rest of the app. All processing happens strictly on your iPhone.

PermissionWhy the app asksWhat happens to the data
Photos To analyze your library locally for similar shots, duplicates, screenshots, blurry photos, Live Photos, heavy formats (PNG/TIFF/BMP/RAW), bursts, large videos, Smart Moments, and Storage X-Ray charts, and to save optimized stills, HEIC files, compressed videos, or exported vault items. Photos and videos are read and processed strictly on your iPhone using Apple Vision, ImageIO, AVFoundation, and PhotoKit. Nothing is ever uploaded. Both Full Access and Limited Access work.
Contacts To find duplicate contact cards so you can merge them, and to list incomplete cards with no phone number or email. Contacts are read locally on your iPhone. Before any merge or delete, Spacewise saves a local .vcf backup inside its private app container so you can restore it from Settings › Contact backups.
Face ID / Touch ID Optional biometric unlock for your encrypted Secret Folder. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS LocalAuthentication. Spacewise only receives a pass/fail result and never accesses biometric templates.
Notifications Only if you turn on the optional weekly review reminder in Settings. The reminder is scheduled locally by iOS on your device. No push notification servers or tokens are used.

4. Data stored on your device

Spacewise stores the following data inside its private sandbox on your iPhone. None of it is transmitted to us or any external service:

  • Encrypted Secret Folder — media files, thumbnails, and the folder manifest are encrypted on disk with 256-bit AES-GCM (CryptoKit), protected with iOS FileProtectionType.complete, and excluded from iCloud and computer backups.
  • Secret Folder PIN verifier & vault key — stored in the iOS Keychain with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly. Your raw PIN digits are never stored; only a salted HKDF-SHA256 verifier is kept. Five wrong tries lock the keypad for 30 seconds.
  • Contact backups — up to 10 recent local .vcf snapshots created before contact merges or deletions.
  • Scan cache, swipe progress & cleanup log — local records so scans resume quickly and you can see how much space you have reclaimed over time, plus the free-space snapshot shared with the Home Screen widget via an App Group on the same iPhone.

Deleting Spacewise from your iPhone permanently erases all local app data, including everything inside the Secret Folder. Export any Secret Folder items you wish to keep back to your Photos library before uninstalling.

5. Network connections

Spacewise makes no network connections. All photo analysis, HEIC/Live Photo optimization, video transcoding, contact deduplication, charting, and AES-256 encryption execute offline on your iPhone. (If iOS Photos downloads an iCloud Photo Library original that you explicitly choose to preview or import into the Secret Folder, that transfer is performed by Apple's system PhotoKit framework under your Apple ID settings.)

6. Third parties

Spacewise contains zero third-party SDKs, zero ad networks, and zero analytics trackers. Separately from the app, Apple provides developers with optional, aggregated App Store statistics and crash logs governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Children

Spacewise is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because Quillpine collects and holds no personal data about you, there is no server-side data to access, export, or erase. Every piece of local state stored by Spacewise resides on your iPhone under your direct control and can be cleared inside the app or by deleting the app.
