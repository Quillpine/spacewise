Spacewise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Spacewise is a storage cleaner for iPhone, published by Quillpine. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Spacewise does not collect any of it. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data.
  • Your photos, videos, contacts, and calendar events are read only on your iPhone and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no account system and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The only network connection the app ever makes is the optional Internet Speed Test.

1. Who we are

Spacewise is developed and published by Quillpine ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, write to trankien.sk.1211@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Quillpine does not operate a server for Spacewise, does not receive any data from the app, and has no way to identify you or your device. We do not build profiles, we do not sell or share data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data the app accesses on your device

Spacewise needs access to parts of your iPhone to do its job. Every permission is requested only when you open the feature that needs it, after a screen explaining why. You can decline any of them, and the rest of the app keeps working. All of the processing below happens entirely on your iPhone.

PermissionWhy the app asksWhat happens to the data
Photos To scan your library for duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos, Live Photos, panoramas, bursts, and large videos. Photos and videos are read and analysed on the device. They are never copied off the device and never sent to us or anyone else. Full access and limited access both work.
Contacts To find duplicate contacts so you can merge them, and to list cards with no phone number or email. Contacts are read on the device. Before any merge or delete, the app writes a .vcf backup into its own private storage on your iPhone so you can undo the change from Settings › Contact backups.
Calendars To list past events so you can delete old ones in bulk. Events are read on the device and only the ones you select are deleted. Nothing is copied or transmitted.
Face ID Optional unlock for the Secret Folder. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app only receives a yes-or-no result. Spacewise never sees or stores your biometric data.
Notifications Only if you turn on the weekly cleaning reminder in Settings. The reminder is scheduled locally by iOS on your device. There are no push notifications and no server involved.

4. Data stored on your device

Spacewise keeps the following in its own private storage on your iPhone. None of it is synced to us or to any cloud service by the app.

  • Secret Folder contents — photos and videos you choose to move there are stored inside the app's private container.
  • Secret Folder PIN — your 4-digit PIN is stored in the iOS Keychain, marked so that it stays on this iPhone only. It is never synced to iCloud and is not included in device backups. Five wrong attempts in a row temporarily lock the keypad.
  • Contact backups — .vcf snapshots written before each contact merge or delete.
  • Cleanup log — a local record of how much space you have freed over time.
  • Speed test history — your last 20 results.
  • App settings — your preferences and the free-space figure shown by the Home Screen widget.

Deleting Spacewise from your iPhone removes all of it. If the Secret Folder still contains items you want to keep, export them back to your photo library before deleting the app.

5. Network connections

Spacewise makes exactly one kind of network connection, and only when you start it yourself:

Internet Speed Test (Tools › Speed Test) downloads and uploads test data to Cloudflare's public speed test service at speed.cloudflare.com in order to measure your connection.

Like any connection to any website, Cloudflare receives your IP address and standard technical connection information. Cloudflare is an independent company and handles that information under its own privacy policy, available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. No photos, contacts, calendar data, or identifiers from your device are included — the transfer is meaningless test data used only to measure speed. The app uses a non-persistent session, so no cookies or cache from the test are kept.

If you never open the Speed Test, Spacewise makes no network connections at all.

6. Third parties

Spacewise contains no third-party software development kits, no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no social media components. Apart from the Cloudflare speed test described above, the app communicates with no external service.

Separately from the app, Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics such as download counts and, if you choose to send them, crash reports. We cannot identify individual users from these. Apple's handling of that information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Children

Spacewise is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content, and no way to communicate with other people.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data a company holds about you. Quillpine holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. The data described in section 4 lives on your iPhone, under your control: you can delete any of it from within the app, or remove all of it at once by deleting the app.
