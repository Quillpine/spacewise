Frequently asked questions

Media Optimizer & Storage

How does Media Optimizer save space without deleting my photos?

Live → Still strips the hidden 3-second motion video clip attached to a Live Photo while saving the exact full-resolution still image (preserving EXIF capture date, GPS coordinates, and Favorite heart). PNG/RAW → HEIC converts heavy uncompressed or lossless files into Apple's efficient HEIC format only when at least 15% space is saved.

In both modes, Spacewise saves the optimized copy to Photos first, then asks whether you want to move the heavier originals to Recently Deleted.

I deleted originals, why didn't my free iPhone storage rise right away?

iOS moves deleted photos and videos into Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted for roughly 30 days as a safety net. To reclaim disk space immediately, open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, and empty that album.

How does Synchronized Zoom Compare work?

Open Similar & Duplicates or Bursts and tap Compare on any group. Pinch or drag on either photo to zoom (up to 5×) and pan both shots in lockstep, inspect their focus sharpness score, megapixel count, codec, and file size, then tap Keep Only A or Keep Only B.

Encrypted Secret Folder

How secure is the Secret Folder?

Every photo, video, thumbnail, and manifest inside the Secret Folder is encrypted on disk using 256-bit AES-GCM (Apple CryptoKit) under FileProtectionType.complete and excluded from backups. Photos are decrypted strictly in memory when viewed, and your PIN is verified via a salted HKDF-SHA256 digest stored only in this iPhone's Keychain.

I forgot my Secret Folder PIN. Can you reset it?

No. Because Spacewise operates 100% offline with zero servers or backdoors, we have no access to your PIN or encryption keys. Deleting the app is the only way to reset the folder, and doing so permanently erases all encrypted items inside it. Always export important items back to Photos before deleting Spacewise or switching iPhones.

Permissions & Contacts

How do I undo a contact merge?

Spacewise automatically saves a local .vcf backup before every merge or delete. Open Settings › Contact backups inside Spacewise to restore a previous snapshot.

Does Spacewise ever connect to the internet or upload my photos?

Never. Spacewise makes zero network connections, has no analytics or ad SDKs, and processes everything locally on your iPhone. Read our Privacy Policy for complete details.
